A poem for the long evening

The Lantern Hour

✦

When daylight folds its paper wings and streets forget the sound of day, I light a lantern small and brave and let it keep the dark at bay.

It is not much—a thread of gold, a quiet room, a cup of tea— but small lights learn the patient art of being more than they can be.

So if your evening wanders far and every window looks the same, remember one unguarded flame is still enough to call your name.
