# Rocket League 1v1 vs Bot - Game Instructions

## How to Play

This is a 2D Rocket League-style game where you play against an AI bot. The goal is to score 3 goals before the bot does!

## Controls

### Desktop
- **Left Arrow / A**: Move left
- **Right Arrow / D**: Move right  
- **Up Arrow / W / Space**: Boost (charge required)

### Mobile
- **Left Button (←)**: Move left
- **Right Button (→)**: Move right
- **Boost Button (⚡)**: Boost

## Game Features

- **Physics**: Realistic car physics with gravity, friction, and momentum
- **Boost System**: Limited boost that charges over time
- **Bot AI**: Difficulty-based AI that tracks the ball
- **Time Limit**: 3 minutes per match
- **Mobile Optimized**: Works on both desktop and mobile devices

## How to Win

Score 3 goals by getting the ball behind the opponent's goal line. The first team to reach 3 goals wins!

## Game Elements

- **Blue Car**: Your player-controlled car
- **Red Car**: AI-controlled bot
- **Gold Ball**: The ball you must control and score with
- **Boost Meters**: Shows available boost for both cars
