# 3D Tony Hawk Skate Park Design

## Overview
A professional 3D skate park designed for a 2-minute trick run sequence, inspired by classic Tony Hawk Pro Skater levels.

## Park Layout for 2-Minute Run

### Phase 1: Warm-up (0:00 - 0:30)
- **Start Position**: Horizontal rail at entrance
- **Trick Sequence**:
  1. Push-up on rail (30 seconds)
  2. Ollie to backside air over low quarter-pipe (15 seconds)
  3. Grab rail slide (15 seconds)

### Phase 2: Main Sequence (0:30 - 1:30)
- **Vertical Ramp Setup**:
  - Flatbar approach
  - 20-inch vertical ramp with K-gap
  - Airbluntslide to pool edge
- **Technical Features**:
  - 3/4pipe manual
  - Bseling onto vertical edge
  - Powerslide transitions

### Phase 3: Finale (1:30 - 2:00)
- **High-Speed Elements**:
  - Hop-over rail (30 seconds)
  - Double-under flip down ramp (20 seconds)
  - Landing grab cleanup (10 seconds)
  - Perfect landing celebration (20 seconds)

## Technical Specifications

### Geometry Requirements
- **Total Park Area**: 50m x 40m
- **Structure Height**: 12 meters max
- **Surface Material**: Polyurethane-coated concrete
- **Safety Features**: Padding on all metal edges

### Ideal Skater Profile
- **Skill Level**: Professional (Tony Hawk style)
- **Deck Size**: 8.0" x 32"
- **Trick Compatibility**: Full trick manual available

## Design Notes

### Key Areas
1. **Entry Plaza**: Wide approach area for initial setup
2. **Vertical Core**: Central 20" ramp with K-gap
3. **Pool Complex**: Half-pipe with grab features
4. **Flatbar Zone**: Technical trick area
5. **Safety Zones**: Designated landing areas

### Line Design
- **Run Flow**: Continuous movement with trick variety
- **Difficulty Progression**: Easy → Medium → Expert
- **Visual Flow**: Photorealistic textures matching Tony Hawk aesthetic

## Rendering Notes

### Visual Style
- **Lighting**: Daytime, cinematic lighting
- **Camera Angles**: Dynamic, skater-eye perspective
- **Texture Quality**: High-poly with realistic wear patterns
- **Animation Style**: Fluid, exaggerated Tony Hawk physics

### Timing Breakdown
- **Total Duration**: 120 seconds
- **Trick Count**: 15+ varied tricks
- **Difficulty Peak**: 45-75 seconds
- **Recovery Time**: Built into finale sequence
